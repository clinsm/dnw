Ducting 
Solutions

DNW Ecotopia provides complete duct manufacturing, supply, installation and maintenance services for HVAC and ventilation systems across South Africa.

We deliver custom-engineered ducting solutions for commercial, industrial, institutional and residential projects. We manufacture, supply, install and maintain rectangular, round, square and spiral ducting together with plenums, bends, reducers, branches, dampers, grilles, diffusers, louvres and extraction fans.

Every system is planned around airflow performance, site conditions, compliance needs and long-term reliability, helping your building move clean air efficiently and safely. We believe that our success is built on strong client relationships, honesty, reliability, and consistently delivering work of the highest standard.
